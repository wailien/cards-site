# Cards – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 16 October 2026**

Cards is a flashcard app for language learning, developed by Alena Air ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## The short version

- Your cards, lists and study progress are stored on your device. There is no Cards server and no account with me.
- Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on a feature that needs it: Google Drive sync, reading a card aloud, or buying Cards Pro.
- The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

Lists, cards, review history, settings and any files you import (Anki, Pleco, CSV) are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Google Sign-In and Google Drive sync (Cards Pro)

If you turn on Drive sync, you sign in with your Google account. The app receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google to show who is signed in. It requests the `drive.file` permission, which only allows access to files the app itself creates. Your data is written to a folder named `cards_app` in your own Google Drive as a file named `cards_app.json`, and read back from there. I never see this data; it moves between your device and your Drive.

Signing out in Settings stops the sync. You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the `cards_app` folder from your Drive.

## Reading cards aloud

When you tap the speaker icon, the card text is spoken using your device's text-to-speech engine. For some languages, if a cloud voice is configured in the build, the text of that card is sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to generate the audio, and the audio is cached on your device. No other card data is sent.

## Purchases

Cards Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. I receive no payment details. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. The app stores only whether Pro is unlocked.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: Drive sync, cloud text-to-speech and Google Play purchases.
- **Files**: only when you choose a file to import or a location to export to, through the system file picker.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
